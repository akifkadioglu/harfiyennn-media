Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Harfiyen is an internal publishing tool. It is used only by the Harfiyen team to upload videos to the team's own TikTok and Instagram accounts. It has no public users and offers no sign-up.

Data we access

When the account owner connects TikTok, we receive an access token, a refresh token and the account's open ID. We use them only to upload videos the account owner has approved and to read the account's basic profile.

Storage and sharing

Tokens are stored on the team's own computer and are never shared, sold or sent to third parties. We do not collect data about viewers or other TikTok users.

Deletion

Access can be revoked at any time in TikTok under Settings → Security → Manage app permissions. To ask for deletion of stored tokens, write to hello@akifkadioglu.dev.
